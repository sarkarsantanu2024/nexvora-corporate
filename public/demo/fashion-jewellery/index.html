Plain facts

What it is, how long it lasts, and the exchange rule.

What it is made of

  • This is imitation jewellery. There is no gold or silver in any piece.
  • Gold-plated means a thin gold-colour polish on brass or alloy.
  • Oxidised means a dark silver-look finish on brass or alloy.
  • AD means American diamond, a machine-cut glass-like stone.
  • Skin reacts to metal? Ask for the nickel-free cards.

How long it lasts

  • Polish on a daily chain lasts 3 to 6 months. On pieces worn a few times a year, much longer.
  • Keep it away from water, sweat and perfume. Put it on last, take it off first.
  • Store each piece in the zip pouch we give, away from air.
  • Loose stone? We fix it at the counter for ₹10.

The exchange rule

  • Exchange within 7 days with the bill, if the piece is unused and on its card.
  • No cash refund. You take another piece or a credit slip valid for 3 months.
  • Nose pins and sale items are not exchanged.
  • Broken in courier? Send an unboxing video within 24 hours and we replace it free.

About the academy

Started in a Lake Market drawing room in 1998

Nupur Nritya Academy began with six students and a harmonium in Sharmila Sen's family home. Today we have two practice halls with sprung wooden floors, five dance styles and students from Kalighat to Jadavpur.

We keep batches small, usually ten to fourteen students, so every child is seen. Beginners spend the first three months on posture, rhythm and the basic adavus or tatkaar before any item is taught.

Students who stay with us sit for Prayag Sangeet Samiti and Bangiya Sangeet Parishad examinations each year. Many have gone on to perform at Rabindra Sadan and Nazrul Mancha.

"A dancer is made by repetition, not by talent. Come regularly and the rest follows."

Our guru

Sharmila Sen

Sharmila learnt Bharatanatyam for fourteen years under Guru Thankamani Kutty in Kolkata, and Kathak at Padatik from 1989 to 1994. She has performed at Dover Lane, Uday Shankar Nrityotsav and in Dhaka and Singapore.

She has run Nupur since 1998 and still teaches every beginner batch herself. Two senior students, Ritika and Anwesha, assist with the children's and Bollywood batches.

  • Sangeet Prabhakar (Bharatanatyam), Prayag Sangeet Samiti
  • Nritya Visharad (Kathak), Bangiya Sangeet Parishad
  • Graded artist, Doordarshan Kolkata, since 2004
  • Examiner for Prayag Sangeet Samiti school-level dance

Annual show

Every student who has attended for six months dances on stage. Costumes, make-up and the hall are arranged by us. Families get four passes each.

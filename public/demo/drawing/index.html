About us

A two-room art school on Sarat Bose Road

Rong Tuli was started in 2009 by Debjani Ghosh, a Government College of Art & Craft graduate, after years of teaching from her verandah. The school now has two rooms with north light, a shelf of 400 reference books and a kettle that is always on.

We teach drawing the old way: look first, then draw. Children start with shapes and shading. Adults start wherever they are. Exam students work on a timed portfolio from day one.

Every student gets a folder. At the end of each term we put the work up on the wall and invite parents in.

What we teach

Five courses, colour-coded

Pick by age and by what you want to make. Each course runs in three-month terms. You can join mid-term and pay for the months left.

Kids' drawing (6–12)

Shapes, shading, colour pencils, oil pastels and poster colour. One finished picture every week to take home. Handwriting improves as a side effect.

Age 6–1290 minTwice a week

Pencil & sketching

Still life, perspective, light and shadow, graphite and charcoal. The foundation for everything else. Good for teens and adults who want to draw "properly".

Age 13+2 hoursTwice a week

Watercolour

Washes, wet-on-wet, Kolkata street scenes and the lakes. We paint outdoors at Rabindra Sarobar one Sunday a month in winter.

Age 14+2 hoursOnce or twice a week

Art college exam prep

For Government College of Art & Craft, Rabindra Bharati, Kala Bhavana and NIFT/NID. Timed object drawing, memory drawing, composition and a 20-sheet portfolio. Mock tests every month.

Class 10–12 & gap year3 hoursThrice a week

Weekend hobby batch (adults)

Saturday afternoons. No exams, no homework. Pencil, pen-and-ink or watercolour, your choice. Most students are 25 to 60 and have not drawn since school.

Age 18+2 hoursSaturdays

Short workshops

Two-day workshops in the school holidays: alpona, Pujo-theme poster, charcoal portrait, urban sketching. Announced on our WhatsApp group a month ahead.

Before your first class

What to bring

All of this is available at the stationery shop below us or at any art shop on College Street. Bring what you have; we lend the rest for the first month.

Kids' drawing

  • A4 drawing copy, 100 pages
  • HB and 2B pencils, eraser, sharpener
  • 24-shade colour pencils
  • Oil pastels, 25 shades
  • An old shirt or apron
We supply paper, poster colour and brushes.

Pencil, sketching, exam prep

  • Cartridge paper, A3 and half-imperial
  • Pencils 2H to 8B, charcoal sticks
  • Kneaded eraser, blending stump
  • Drawing board 18 x 24 inch with clips
  • Fixative spray (exam prep only)
Exam students also need a portfolio bag before the mock tests.

Watercolour & hobby

  • Watercolour paper, 300 gsm, A4 pad
  • 12-pan watercolour set
  • Round brushes no. 2, 6, 10 and a 1-inch flat
  • Two water jars, cloth rag
  • Masking tape
A student-grade set costs about ₹450 to ₹700.
How it works

A term at Rong Tuli

Three months, one theme, one wall show at the end. This is the same for a six-year-old and a sixty-year-old.

  1. Trial class

    Come for one free class. We watch how you hold the pencil and suggest a batch. No admission fee for the trial.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4

    Basics for the term's theme: lines, shapes, tone. Everyone draws the same still life so progress is easy to see.

  3. Weeks 5 to 10

    Your own work. Teacher sits with each student for ten minutes every class. Corrections are drawn on tracing paper, never on your sheet.

  4. Wall show

    Last Sunday of the term. Work goes up on the studio wall, parents and friends come, tea is served. Then a new term begins.

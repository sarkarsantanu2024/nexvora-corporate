Custom orders

Draw it on paper. We will make it in wood.

About half our work is made to order: a bed that fits an odd corner, an almirah that goes up to the ceiling, a dining table for a family of ten. This is how it goes, start to finish.

  1. Day 1

    Measure

    We come to your home with a tape and a notebook, or you send measurements and photos on WhatsApp.

  2. Day 2 to 3

    Drawing and quote

    A sketch with sizes, the wood, the polish shade and a fixed price. Nothing changes after you say yes.

  3. Day 4

    Pick the wood

    Come to the workshop and choose the planks yourself if you like. 50% advance to begin.

  4. Week 1 to 4

    Making

    Cut, joined and assembled by hand. We send photos every week so you see it take shape.

  5. Week 5

    Polish

    Three coats of melamine or PU, sanded between each. You approve the shade on a sample first.

  6. Week 6

    Delivery and fitting

    Our own men deliver, assemble and fit in your room. Balance paid when you are happy.

The workshop

Behind the showroom, eleven carpenters and a wood yard.

Nirmal Bosu started with two carpenters in a tin shed in 1979. The shed is now a 4,000 sq ft workshop, but the way of working has not changed. Wood is bought in planks, stacked and air-dried in our own yard for at least eight months before anyone cuts it. That is why our doors do not warp in the monsoon.

Joints are mortise and tenon, glued and pegged. No nails in the frame, no particle board hidden behind a veneer. Open any drawer and you will see wood all the way through.

After it is in your home

We stay responsible for what we make.

  • Ten-year polish warranty

    If the polish peels, cracks or clouds in ten years, we re-polish it free. Scratches from use are repaired at cost.

  • Repair and re-polish

    Grandmother’s almirah, a wobbly dining chair, a bed with a cracked plank. Bring it in or we collect it. Most repairs done in a week.

  • Shifting house?

    We dismantle, move and refit our furniture when you change flats, within Kolkata, for a flat ₹1,500 per piece.

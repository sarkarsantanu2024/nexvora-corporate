Documents for ITR

What to send for your income tax return.

Photograph each one on your phone and send it on WhatsApp. Not everything applies to everyone; send what you have and we ask for the rest.

  • PAN and Aadhaar (linked)
  • Form 16 from your employer
  • Bank statements, 1 April to 31 March
  • Interest certificates from banks and post office
  • Home loan statement (principal and interest)
  • LIC, PPF, ELSS and tuition fee receipts
  • Health insurance premium receipt
  • Rent receipts if you claim HRA
  • Broker capital gains statement (shares, mutual funds)
  • Sale deed and purchase deed if you sold property
  • Last year's ITR acknowledgement
  • Bank account number and IFSC for the refund

We download your Form 26AS and AIS from the portal ourselves and match every entry against your papers, so nothing the department already knows about is left out of the return.

How we work

Four steps, mostly on your phone.

  1. Tell us what you need

    A WhatsApp message or a visit. We ask a few questions and tell you the fee and the papers needed.

  2. Send the papers

    Photos on WhatsApp are fine. Originals only where the government asks for them. We confirm receipt the same day.

  3. Check before filing

    You get a draft to approve: the return, the application or the statement. Nothing is filed until you say yes.

  4. Filed, with proof

    Acknowledgement, certificate or challan sent to you on WhatsApp and email. Kept in your file at the office too.

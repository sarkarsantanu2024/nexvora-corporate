About us

A ground-floor house with a garden, run by two sisters

Chotto Pakhi means "little bird". We opened in 2014 in our family house on Jodhpur Park with eleven children. Today there are sixty, in three small classes, taught by six teachers and two helpers who have all been with us for more than four years.

We follow a play-based plan: every week has a theme (rain, vegetables, the post office) and every lesson is a game, a song or something to make with your hands. Reading and writing start gently in KG, so children walk into Class 1 at any Kolkata school without fear.

We speak English and Bengali in class, and a little Hindi where a child needs it.

A day at school

What a Tuesday looks like

The same shape every day, so children know what comes next. Nursery and KG timings shown; Playgroup starts half an hour later.

  1. 9:00 am

    Arrival and free play

    Shoes off, bags on hooks. Blocks, puzzles and the book corner while everyone arrives.

  2. 9:30 am

    Circle time

    Good-morning song, weather, the day's theme. Everyone gets to say one thing.

  3. 10:00 am

    Learning through play

    Letters, numbers or the theme, taught with games and things to touch. Twenty-five minutes, then a change.

  4. 10:30 am

    Tiffin

    Children eat from home. We wash hands together and sit at the long table. No chips or chocolate, please.

  5. 11:00 am

    Garden or music

    Slides and swings in the garden on dry days, music and movement indoors in the rains.

  6. 11:40 am

    Art and craft

    Paint, clay, paper tearing, pasting. Aprons on. Something goes home in the bag most days.

  7. 12:15 pm

    Story and goodbye

    One story on the mat, then the goodbye song. Children are handed only to the adult on the pick-up card.

  8. 12:30 pm

    Day-care continues

    For children staying on: lunch, a nap on their own mat, quiet play. Pick-up any time till 4 pm.

Safety

What we do to keep children safe

Plain facts, not promises. Ask to see any of this when you visit.

CCTV in every room

Six cameras including the garden and the gate. Recordings are kept for 30 days. Parents can ask to see footage.

Pick-up card

Each child has two photo cards. We hand a child only to the person holding one. Phone us before sending anyone else.

First aid and a doctor nearby

Two teachers are first-aid trained. Dr Sengupta's clinic is 200 metres away and on call during school hours.

Filtered water, clean toilets

RO water, child-height toilets and basins, cleaned twice a day. Soap, not sanitiser, for small hands.

Ground floor only

No stairs for children. Rounded furniture edges, socket covers, gates on both doors, a soft floor under the slide.

Same faces every day

All staff have been with us four years or more, with police verification on file. No substitute teachers from outside.

Small on purpose

Sixty children. Every one of them known by name.

We have never taken more than twenty children in a class, and we do not plan to. A teacher who knows that Rudra skips breakfast and Aarohi is scared of the slide is worth more than a bigger building.

Admissions 2026–27

How to join

The session starts in April. We take children all year if a seat is free. There is no test for the child and no interview for parents, only a visit.

  1. Message or call us

    Tell us your child's date of birth and which class you are thinking of. We will suggest a day to visit.

  2. Visit during school hours

    Come between 10 and 11:30 am with your child. See a class, meet the teacher, sit in the garden. Takes about forty minutes.

  3. Fill the form

    A two-page form and the documents listed here. We confirm the seat the same day if one is free.

  4. Settling-in week

    Short days for the first week, a parent nearby. Most children are happy to stay alone by day four or five.

What to bring for admission

  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Four passport photos of the child
  • One photo each of both parents and the pick-up person
  • Vaccination card (copy)
  • Address proof of a parent
Admission ₹5,000 and the first quarter's fee are paid on the day of admission. UPI or cash.

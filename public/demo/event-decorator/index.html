How it works

From your first message to the last chair going back on the van.

  1. 30 days before

    Visit and quotation

    We see the venue, measure the stage and gate, and send a written list with prices. You pay nothing at this stage.

  2. 7 days before

    Booking and advance

    Pick the flowers and colours from our photo book. 50% advance confirms the date. We buy flowers to order, so this is the last day to change the design.

  3. The day before

    Pandal and lights go up

    Our team builds the pandal, lays carpet, hangs lights and tests the sound. You check everything the night before.

  4. The day

    Flowers at dawn, cleared by night

    Fresh flowers arrive from Mullick Ghat before 6 am and the stage is ready by 11. After the event we take everything away. The balance is paid that evening.

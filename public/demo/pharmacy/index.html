Home delivery

Three steps, no app to install.

  1. Send the prescription

    A clear photo on WhatsApp, with your address and the number of days you need. Old prescriptions for regular medicines are fine.

  2. We confirm the bill

    The pharmacist checks the stock and sends the total with each item listed. If a brand is missing we suggest a substitute and wait for your yes.

  3. Delivery in two hours

    Our own delivery boy, in a sealed bag with the printed bill. Pay by cash or UPI at the door. Returns of unopened strips within three days.

Delivery areas: Behala, Sakher Bazar, Parnasree, Barisha, Thakurpukur, James Long Sarani and Taratala. Beyond that, ₹40 per delivery, same day.

Monthly medicine box

For parents who live alone and children who live far away.

Many of our customers are sons and daughters in Bangalore, Pune or abroad, paying for a mother in Behala. The box takes the monthly worry out of it.

  • 1

    Send the prescription once

    We keep it on file and note the doctor's review date. When the prescription is due to be renewed we remind you two weeks before.

  • 2

    Packed by time of day

    Strips are cut and sorted into a weekly pill organiser: morning, afternoon, night. Each medicine's name and purpose is written in Bengali and English on the lid.

  • 3

    Delivered before the last dose

    We deliver on a fixed date each month, three days before the previous box runs out. The delivery boy checks the BP if asked and sends you the reading.

  • 4

    One bill, paid from anywhere

    A UPI link or bank transfer each month, with a photo of the bill and the organiser. No advance, cancel any month.

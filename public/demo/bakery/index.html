How to order

Four messages and your cake is booked.

  1. Send the details

    Flavour, weight, the name to write, and the date. A photo of a design you like if you have one. Eggless or not.

  2. We confirm the price

    You get the price, a sketch for theme cakes, and a pickup time. Pay ₹200 advance by UPI, or the full amount for cakes above 2 kg.

  3. We bake it tomorrow

    Cakes are baked in the morning and finished in the afternoon. We send you a photo before packing so you can ask for a change.

  4. Pick up or delivery

    Collect from the shop after 4 pm, or we deliver within 5 km for ₹60 (free above ₹1,500). Cakes travel in a cold box.

Questions people ask

Before you order.

  • Can I get a cake today?

    Only a plain half-kg chocolate or vanilla, if one is on the counter. Everything else needs an order by 6 pm the day before. For a theme or tier cake, three days.

  • Which areas do you deliver to?

    Dum Dum, Nagerbazar, Lake Town, Baguiati, Belgharia, Sinthee and Dum Dum Cantonment. Roughly 5 km from the shop. Delivery is ₹60, free on orders above ₹1,500.

  • Are the eggless cakes really eggless?

    Yes. They are baked in a separate oven with separate tins and bowls. If you need it on a day we do not bake eggless, tell us and we will arrange it.

  • Does the photo cake taste of paper?

    No. The sheet is made of sugar and starch and melts into the cream within an hour. Faces print best on light coloured cream.

  • How long does a cake keep?

    Fresh cream cakes should be eaten within 24 hours and kept in the fridge. Fondant cakes keep for two days. Take the cake out 30 minutes before cutting.

  • What if I need to cancel?

    Cancel before we start baking (by 8 am on the day) and the advance comes back to you. After that the advance is kept because the cake is already made.

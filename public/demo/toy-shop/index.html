Safety

What we check before a toy leaves the shop.

  • BIS (ISI) mark on the box

    We stock only toys that carry the mark. Ask us and we show you where it is printed.

  • Batteries checked at the counter

    We put the cells in and switch the toy on in front of you. If it does not run, it does not get billed.

  • Right age for the toy

    We read the age on the box and tell you if a toy has small parts not meant for a child under 3.

  • Box opened if you ask

    Check the edges, the wheels and the stitching yourself before you pay.

  • Non-toxic crayons and clay

    The art shelf has only colours marked non-toxic on the pack.

  • Cycles fitted and brakes tested

    Our mechanic fits the cycle, sets the seat to the child’s height and tests both brakes.

Questions

Asked at the counter every day.

Where do you deliver?

Within 3 km of the shop: Kasba, Rajdanga, Bosepukur, Haltu, Ruby and Picnic Garden. Same day if you order before 7 pm. ₹30 per delivery, free above ₹799. Cycles are delivered fitted.

Is gift wrapping really free?

Yes, for every toy bought here, one piece or eighty. You choose the paper. A small gift tag is free too.

Do toys come with batteries?

Some do. If the box says batteries not included, we tell you before billing and you can add cells at the counter (from ₹20 a pair). Either way we test the toy before it is packed.

How do I pay?

Cash, UPI and all cards at the shop. For home delivery: UPI in advance, or cash and UPI at the door.

Can I exchange a gift someone else bought here?

Yes, if you have the bill or the gift slip we put inside the wrapping, and it is within 7 days.

Do you repair cycles and remote-control toys?

Cycles bought here get one free service in the first three months. Remote-control toys have a 7 day replacement; after that we can only check the battery and charger.

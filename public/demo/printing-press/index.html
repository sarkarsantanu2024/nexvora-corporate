Same-day printing

Send by 11, collect by 7. Here is how the day goes.

For visiting cards, pamphlets, letterheads, stickers and flex up to 6 by 10 feet. Bill books, wedding cards and anything with spot UV or foil need two days or more, and we say so when you send the file.

  1. By 11 am

    File reaches us

    On WhatsApp, email or a pen drive at the counter. We check size, bleed and resolution within the hour.

  2. By 12 noon

    Proof sent to you

    A photo or PDF proof on WhatsApp. You reply "ok" or ask for a change. Nothing prints before your ok.

  3. 12 to 4 pm

    Printing

    Digital jobs go straight to the machine. Flex prints on the wide-format printer. Offset runs are planned for the next morning.

  4. 4 to 6 pm

    Cutting and finishing

    Lamination, cutting, eyelets, framing. Every job is counted and checked before it is packed.

  5. By 7 pm

    Ready to collect

    We message you when it is packed. Collect at the counter or ask for delivery the same evening within 5 km.

Sending files

What a print-ready file looks like.

You do not need to know any of this if you want us to design the job. If you have your own designer, give them this list and the job will print right the first time.

  • PDF
    PDF first, then CDR, AI or high-resolution JPGA PDF with fonts embedded is the safest. CorelDRAW files up to version 2024 and Illustrator files are fine. Word and PowerPoint files are not; we will redo them and charge for design.
  • 300
    300 dpi at the actual print sizeA visiting card file should be 3.5 by 2 inches at 300 dpi. A WhatsApp-forwarded image is usually 72 dpi and will print blurry. For flex, 72 to 100 dpi at full size is enough.
  • 3mm
    3 mm bleed on every sideIf your design has colour to the edge, extend it 3 mm beyond the cut line. Keep text and logos at least 3 mm inside the cut line.
  • CMYK
    CMYK colour, not RGBScreens show RGB, presses print CMYK. Bright blues and greens shift the most. If an exact colour matters, come and see a printed swatch.
  • Aa
    Fonts converted to curves, or embeddedEspecially for Bengali text. If the font is missing on our machine the letters change. Convert to outlines or send the font file.
  • #
    Tell us quantity, paper and finish in the messageOne line is enough: "500 visiting cards, both sides, matte". We quote within the hour on working days.

Hygiene

Five things we do for every customer

You should not have to ask. These are the rules in our parlour, and you are welcome to watch us follow them.

  1. 1

    A fresh towel and cape, every time

    Washed at 60°C after one use. Nothing is reused between customers.

  2. 2

    Steel tools go in the steriliser

    Scissors, tweezers, combs and nail tools sit in the UV cabinet between customers.

  3. 3

    Wax is never double-dipped

    One spatula, one dip, in the bin. Strips come out of a sealed pack in front of you.

  4. 4

    Sealed products, opened in front of you

    Facial kits and colour tubes are opened at your chair. The empty pack goes home with you if you want it.

  5. 5

    Ladies-only staff and floor

    No men on the floor at any time, including delivery. The door is curtained from the street.

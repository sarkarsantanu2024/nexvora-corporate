Booking a wedding

Four steps from the first message to the album.

  1. Send the dates

    Message the wedding and reception dates. We tell you the same day whether we are free and which package fits.

  2. Meet at the studio

    Come and see three real albums, not a brochure. Pick the package. Fifty percent books the date; we give a written receipt.

  3. The days themselves

    We reach an hour before the first ritual. Thirty photos reach your family WhatsApp group that night.

  4. Ten days later

    All edited photos on a pen drive and a private link. Album proofs for approval, then the printed album within three weeks.

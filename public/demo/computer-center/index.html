We are not a franchise. The same two teachers have been here since 2011 and know which mistakes a beginner makes in week one.

Certificate

A certificate that says what you can do, with a number anyone can check

Every course ends with a practical exam on the computer. Pass, and you get a printed certificate with a unique ID. An employer can message us the ID and we confirm it the same day.

  • Practical exam, not a written one. You do the tasks on screen in 60 minutes.
  • Grade printed on the certificate: A, B or C, with your typing speed where relevant.
  • One free re-sit within a month if you do not pass the first time.
  • We also prepare students for the NIELIT CCC exam and help with the registration form. The exam fee is paid to NIELIT directly.

Common questions

I have never touched a computer. Can I join?
Yes. Basic Computing starts from switching the machine on. About a third of our students are complete beginners, many over 50.
Do I need my own laptop?
No. Every student has a computer in class and free practice hours. If you do have a laptop, bring it on Saturdays and we will set it up the same way.
Can I pay in instalments?
Two instalments for any course over ₹3,000: half at joining, half after the first month. Cash or UPI. A receipt is given for every payment.
What if I miss classes?
Sit in another slot the same week for the same lesson. If you miss more than a week, we give one free catch-up session.
Is the certificate government recognised?
It is our own certificate with a verifiable ID; it is not a government certificate. For a government one we prepare you for the NIELIT CCC exam, which you sit at a NIELIT centre.
Do you help with jobs?
We do not promise placement. We do fix your resume, run mock interviews for accounts and data entry roles, and pass on openings that local shops and offices send us.

Caterer and decoration

Yours or ours. Both are fine.

Food: in-house or your own caterer

Our in-house caterer serves Bengali and North Indian menus from ₹550 a plate (veg) and ₹750 (non-veg), with a tasting before you book. If you have a family caterer, bring them. There is no charge for an outside caterer and no commission.

The caterer gets the kitchen area from 6 am on the day. Cooking on open fire is allowed in the kitchen yard only. The caterer takes their rubbish away.

How booking works

Five steps from a message to the wedding day.

  1. Send the date

    WhatsApp the date, the event and the guest count. We tell you which hall is free and the rate.

  2. See the hall

    Walk in any day between 10 and 7. If there is an event on, we show you the other hall and the rooms.

  3. Pay the advance

    50% of the hall rate holds the date. UPI, cheque or bank transfer. You get a receipt and a booking letter.

  4. Plan the day

    A month before, we sit with you and your caterer and decorator for an hour and write down the timings.

  5. Settle after

    The balance and any extra hours are paid the next morning. The deposit comes back within 3 days.

Cancellation: the advance is returned in full if you cancel 90 days ahead, half if 30 days ahead, and kept after that. If we can re-book the date, you get it back anyway.

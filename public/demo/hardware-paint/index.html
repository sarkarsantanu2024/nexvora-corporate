For contractors and painters

Site rates for anyone buying for a site.

Thirty-odd painters and masons in Behala buy from us every week. Here is what they get that a walk-in customer does not.

  • 8 to 12 percent off the counter price

    On paint above 40 litres, wire above 5 coils, cement above 50 bags. The rate is written on the bill, not whispered.

  • One GST bill for the whole site

    Give us the owner's or the firm's GST number once. Every delivery goes on the same account and one bill comes at the end of the month.

  • Delivery straight to the floor

    Our van and two helpers carry cement and paint to the floor you are working on, up to the fourth floor without a lift.

  • Return what is unopened

    Sealed tins and unopened bags come back within 15 days for full credit. Tinted shades are the exception, since nobody else will buy them.

  • Fifteen days credit after the third job

    New contractors pay on delivery. Once we have done three sites together, the account runs on fifteen days credit.

How a bulk order works

  1. Send the list

    WhatsApp the item list with quantities, the site address and the floor. A photo of the engineer's list is fine.

  2. Get the rate in an hour

    We reply with the site rate, the delivery day and the total. Nothing moves until you say yes.

  3. Van arrives, you sign

    Count the bags at the gate, sign the challan, and the material goes up. Payment on delivery or on account.

Common questions

Things people ask before they come.

Can you match a shade from a photo or an old wall?
Yes. A chip of the old paint is best, a swatch card from any company is fine, and a photo works for most shades. The machine takes about twenty minutes. We write the shade code on your tin so the same colour can be mixed again later.
Do you deliver, and what does it cost?
Our van delivers twice a day inside Behala. It is free on bills above ₹3,000 and ₹150 below that. Nearby areas such as Thakurpukur, Joka and New Alipore are ₹250 flat. Cement, sand and bricks are always delivered.
How long does cement or sand take?
We do not keep cement in the shop. Order today and it comes from the depot within 48 hours, usually next day. Sand and stone chips take two to three days because the truck is shared with other orders. Minimum 10 bags of cement for delivery.
Can I return paint I did not use?
Sealed tins of white or ready shades, yes, within 15 days with the bill. Tinted shades mixed for you cannot be returned because nobody else will buy that colour. Opened tins cannot be returned either.
Do you give a GST bill?
Every sale gets a printed bill with our GST number. If you want the bill in a firm's name with its GST number, tell us before billing and it goes on the bill.
Do you give credit to contractors?
Not on the first job. After three sites bought and paid on time, a contractor's account runs on fifteen days credit with a monthly statement. Walk-in customers pay at the counter, by cash, UPI or card.

Read this before you book

What the price covers, and what it does not.

The same list applies to every package above. We write it on the quote too.

Included

  • Hotel on twin sharing, with breakfast
  • Train or bus ticket from Kolkata and back, as listed in the rate table
  • Car for all sightseeing days named in the itinerary
  • Station or airport pick-up and drop at the destination
  • Permits (Sikkim) and ferry tickets (Andaman)
  • All taxes. A phone number that answers while you are travelling

Not included

  • Lunch and dinner, unless the rate table says so
  • Flights (Andaman), shown separately on the quote
  • Entry tickets, boat rides, ropeway, camera fees
  • Porter, tips, laundry, room service, mineral water
  • Anything caused by a landslide, strike, train cancellation or weather
  • Travel insurance (we can arrange it, see below)

How to book

Four steps. The first two cost nothing.

  1. Tell us dates and people

    WhatsApp or walk in. Give the destination, travel dates, number of adults and children, and whether you want train or flight.

  2. Get a written quote

    Within one working day you get a quote per person, with the hotel name, the train number and the included and not-included list.

  3. Pay 50% to confirm

    UPI, bank transfer or cash at the office. We book the tickets and the hotel the same day and send you the confirmations.

  4. Pay the rest, then travel

    Balance is due 7 days before departure. You get a printed itinerary, driver's number and our 24-hour number for the trip.

Questions people ask

Before you send the advance.

What if I cancel?

More than 30 days before travel: full refund minus what the railway or airline keeps. 15 to 30 days: we keep 25% of the package. Under 15 days: we keep 50%. Under 72 hours: no refund, because the hotel does not refund us either. Every refund is paid within 7 working days.

What do children pay?

Under 5 years, sharing the parents' bed: free, you only pay the train or flight ticket if a seat is needed. 5 to 11 years with an extra bed: 60% of the adult price. 12 and above: adult price.

What if the train ticket is waitlisted?

We book the day the window opens, 60 days ahead, so most tickets confirm. If a ticket is still waitlisted 7 days before, we tell you and offer a bus, a different train, or a full refund of that package. We never make you travel on a waitlisted ticket.

Can I change the dates or the hotel?

Yes. Every package can run on your own dates for 2 people or more. You can pick a different hotel from our list, or name one you know; we quote the difference. Honeymoon rooms and ground-floor rooms for elderly travellers are arranged on request.

How do I pay, and do I get a receipt?

UPI, bank transfer, debit card at the office, or cash. Every payment gets a printed receipt with a GST invoice. We do not take payment on any personal UPI number; the name on the UPI is always Bhraman Bilash Tours and Travels.

Vocal

Two kinds of singing, two teachers, one floor.

Vocal classes are taught sitting on the floor with a harmonium and a tanpura app, the way most of us learnt. A child who cannot yet hold a note sits beside a student preparing for a stage.

Rabindra Sangeet

Taught from the Swarabitan notation, song by song, with the words explained before the tune. Students learn to sing with the correct pronunciation and without ornament, as the songs were written to be sung.

Beginners start with the simpler Puja and Prakriti songs. By the second year most students can sing twenty songs from notation and perform at Pochishe Boishakh.

  • Age 6 and up
  • Tue, Thu or Sat, Sun
  • Teacher: Madhumita Sen
  • Board exam prep included

Hindustani classical vocal

Sargam, paltas and alankars for the first six months, whatever the age. Then raag by raag: Yaman, Bhupali, Bhairav, Kafi, in bandish and in alaap. Tabla accompaniment at every Saturday class.

Students who want to sing Bengali adhunik, Nazrul Geeti or film songs are welcome. Classical training is the base; what you sing with it is your choice.

  • Age 8 and up, adults welcome
  • Mon closed, other days
  • Teacher: Pt. Subhankar Roy
  • Board exam prep included
Instruments

Five instruments, taught one student at a time within the batch.

Instrument batches are four to six students. The teacher goes round, listens to each one, corrects the hand, moves on. Bring your own instrument from the second month; we have school ones for the first.

  • Guitar

    Acoustic first, then electric if you wish. Chords, strumming patterns, Bengali and Hindi songs, then scales and lead.

    Age 9+ · Mon closed

  • Keyboard

    Both hands from the first month. Staff notation taught alongside, so students can read Western pieces and play harmonium parts.

    Age 7+ · Wed, Fri, Sun

  • Tabla

    Teentaal, Dadra, Kaharwa, Jhaptaal. Bols spoken before they are played. Our tabla students accompany the vocal classes on Saturdays.

    Age 7+ · Tue, Thu, Sat

  • Harmonium

    For singers who want to accompany themselves, and for anyone who wants to play at home. Scales, chords and song accompaniment.

    Any age · Any day

  • Violin

    Indian style, held low, taught on the Hindustani raag system. Bowing and tuning take the first three months. Patience is required.

    Age 8+ · Wed, Sat

Exam preparation is optional. Plenty of students learn for ten years and never sit an exam. That is also a fine way to learn music.

Music board exams

Year by year, from Prathama to Sangeet Visharad level.

Students who want a certificate sit for music board exams every year from the second year. We prepare them for the syllabus of the boards most Kolkata schools use. Here is what each stage looks like.

  1. Yr 1

    No exam. Just learning.

    Posture, breath, sargam or hand position. We do not put a six-year-old in front of an examiner.

  2. Yr 2–3

    Junior years

    Short theory paper and a practical of two or three pieces. Most children pass comfortably. Viva questions are practised in class from October.

  3. Yr 4–5

    Middle years

    Notation writing, raag identification, history of the form. Practical gets longer. Mock exams in January with teachers from other schools as examiners.

  4. Yr 6+

    Senior and diploma level

    Full performance plus a written paper. Students at this level usually teach the junior batches with us, which is the best preparation there is.

Annual function

Sur Sandhya, the second Sunday of January.

Every student who has attended for six months sings or plays on stage. Juniors in groups, seniors solo, the tabla batch accompanies everyone. We hire the hall, the sound and the lights. Families get three passes each and the rest are open to the neighbourhood.

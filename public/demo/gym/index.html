About the gym

A para gym,
not a mall gym.

Iron Para Gym opened in 2016 on the ground floor of a house in Jodhpur Park. The owner, Subrata Ghosh, still runs the morning shift himself.

It is one big room. Free weights on one side, machines and cardio on the other, a mat area at the back for classes and stretching. There is a fan over every rack and an AC that works in summer.

  • Plates up to 20 kg, dumbbells to 40 kg. Two squat racks, a flat and incline bench, a Smith machine.
  • Trainers on the floor, not at the desk. Ask anyone in a black T-shirt for help with form.
  • No sales pressure. Plans are on this page. Pay for the month you want, nothing more.
  • Women's hour 11 am to 1 pm. Women only on the floor, Monday to Saturday.

What's inside

Everything you need. Nothing you'll pay for and never use.

All of this is included in every membership. Group classes too.

01

Free weights

Two squat racks, flat and incline benches, a deadlift platform, dumbbells from 2.5 to 40 kg.

02

Machines

Cable crossover, lat pulldown, leg press, leg curl and extension, chest press, Smith machine.

03

Cardio & functional

Four treadmills, two cross-trainers, two bikes, a rower, battle ropes, kettlebells and a tyre.

04

Group classes

Zumba, yoga, HIIT and a Sunday stretch class on the mat area. Timetable below. Included.

05

Personal training

One trainer, one plan written for you, twelve sessions a month. Booked in advance so you get the slot you want.

06

Changing room & lockers

Separate changing rooms for men and women, 40 day-lockers (bring your own lock), a shower, filtered drinking water, first-aid box at the desk.

We do not have a steam room, a pool or a juice bar. We keep the fees low instead.

Your first day

How it goes

  1. 1

    Walk in, any open hour

    No appointment. Your first visit is free. Say you are new at the desk.

  2. 2

    Ten minutes with a trainer

    We ask what you want, check for old injuries, measure height, weight and waist.

  3. 3

    A tour and a first workout

    The trainer shows you every machine and walks you through a light first session.

  4. 4

    Pick a plan, or don't

    Pay at the desk if you want to continue. Nobody will call you if you don't.

Bring and know

  • Indoor shoes. Outdoor shoes stay in the rack at the door.
  • A towel and a water bottle. We have filtered water, not disposable cups.
  • A lock for the day-locker. Lockers are emptied at closing.
  • Re-rack your plates and wipe the bench after use.
  • Phone calls off the floor, please. Photos are fine if nobody else is in them.
  • Tell a trainer about any injury, heart condition or pregnancy before starting.
  • Children under 14 are not allowed on the floor.

What we do

Every job a two-wheeler or car needs, under one shed.

01

Scooter and bike servicing

General service with engine oil change, air filter clean, chain and brake adjustment, carburettor or injector clean, and a full wash. We work on Honda, TVS, Bajaj, Hero, Suzuki, Yamaha and Royal Enfield.

  • Engine oil and filter
  • Brake shoes and pads
  • Clutch plates and cable
  • Battery test and replacement
  • Tyre and tube change
  • Chain sprocket kit

General service: about 2 hours. Clutch or sprocket: half a day.

02

Car servicing and AC

Periodic service for hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs: Maruti, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota, Kia. Oil and filters, brake clean, coolant and brake fluid top-up, scanner check for warning lights, wash and vacuum.

  • Engine oil, oil and air filter
  • AC gas top-up and cooling coil clean
  • Brake pads and discs
  • Clutch set and bearing
  • Battery, alternator, starter
  • Wheel alignment and balancing

General service: same day, drop by 10 am and collect by 6 pm. AC service: 3 hours.

03

Tyres, battery and brakes

We stock MRF, CEAT, Apollo and JK tyres for two-wheelers and cars, and Exide and Amaron batteries. Fitting, balancing and alignment on the spot. Brake work uses company pads and shoes, never loose ones.

  • Tubeless puncture repair
  • Tyre fitting and balancing
  • Wheel alignment (cars)
  • Battery with warranty card
  • Brake pads, shoes, discs
  • Brake oil bleed

Tyre or battery change: 30 to 45 minutes. Brake pads: 1 to 2 hours.

04

Denting, painting and wash

Scratches, dents, bumper cracks and full repaint in a dust-free booth with the original shade code. Scooter panels too. Every job ends with a wash, and cars get an interior vacuum.

  • Per-panel dent and paint
  • Bumper repair
  • Full body repaint
  • Scooter panel paint
  • Headlamp polishing
  • Foam wash and vacuum

One or two panels: 3 days. Full repaint: 7 to 10 days. Wash: 40 minutes.

Free with every service

The 10-point check.

Done on every vehicle that comes in, whether for a full service or a puncture. You get a photo of the sheet on WhatsApp with anything that needs attention marked in red. No charge, no pressure to fix it here.

  1. Engine oil level and colourLow or black oil is the first sign of trouble
  2. Brake pad and shoe thicknessMeasured, not guessed
  3. Tyre tread depth and pressureAll tyres, including the spare
  4. Battery voltage and terminalsLoad test, terminals cleaned
  5. All lights and hornHeadlamp, indicators, brake light, number plate light
  6. Chain or belt conditionSlack, wear, lubrication
  7. Coolant and brake fluidLevel and condition (cars)
  8. Suspension and steering playShockers, bushes, tie rods
  9. Clutch and accelerator cablesFree play and fraying
  10. Leaks underneathOil, coolant, fuel

Free   Even if you only come for a puncture or a wash.

Three things customers ask about

Pick-up, spares and insurance.

  • Pick-up and drop

    Our rider collects your scooter or bike, or a driver collects your car, from your home or office between 9 am and 11 am and brings it back by evening. Within 6 km of the garage: Behala, Thakurpukur, Joka, Taratala, New Alipore, Sakherbazar. Charges are in the price table.

  • Genuine spares

    Company parts from authorised distributors, with the box and the bill. If a genuine part is out of stock we tell you, and you choose between waiting and a named aftermarket brand. We do not fit unmarked parts.

  • Insurance claim help

    Had an accident? Bring the car or bike in. We photograph the damage, prepare the estimate in the format the insurer wants, and deal with the surveyor who comes to inspect. Cashless with most major insurers; for others we give you every paper for reimbursement.

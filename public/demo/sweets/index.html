Our story

Three generations behind one counter.

Mohini Mohan Das opened this shop in 1962 with a single karai and a recipe for rasgulla he learnt in Nadia. His son added sandesh and mishti doi in the eighties. Today his grandson runs the kitchen, and the rasgulla is still made the same way, in the same brass karai, with the same morning milk from Barasat.

We do not use preservatives or colour. Whatever is on the counter was made today. What does not sell by night is given away, never kept.

Weddings and events

Sweets for 50 guests or 1,500.

We have supplied sweets for weddings, annaprashans and office pujas across North Kolkata for forty years. Three steps and it is done.

  1. Tell us the date and the count

    Message or call with the date, number of guests and roughly what you have in mind. We reply the same day with a list and a price.

  2. Taste and fix the menu

    Come to the shop and taste. Pick 4 to 8 items. We take a 50% advance and the rest on delivery.

  3. Delivered on the morning

    Our own van delivers within Kolkata from 6 am. Sweets are packed in food-grade boxes, with mishti doi in clay pots.

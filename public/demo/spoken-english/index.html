Levels

From the first sentence to the interview chair.

Everyone gives a short speaking test on the demo day. We put you in the level that fits, not in whichever batch has a seat.

  1. Beginner

    For those who cannot form a sentence

    Everyday words, simple present and past, asking and answering questions. Spoken in class from day one, reading and writing kept small.

  2. Intermediate

    Can speak, but slowly and with mistakes

    Tenses sorted out, common mistakes Bengali speakers make, longer answers, telling a story, describing your work and your day.

  3. Fluent conversation

    Speaks, but wants to sound natural

    Group discussion, debate, telephone English, pronunciation of the sounds we usually get wrong, idioms people actually use.

  4. Interview-ready

    Has a job interview or campus placement coming

    Self-introduction, HR questions, body language, dress, email writing, mock interview on camera with playback. Personality development is in this level.

Inside a class

You talk for forty minutes. The teacher talks for twenty.

Most people who come to us have studied English for ten years in school and still cannot order food in it. The missing piece is not grammar. It is practice, in front of people, with someone correcting you kindly.

  • Every student speaks in every classTen seats means ten turns. Nobody hides at the back.
  • Mistakes corrected after you finish, not duringYou complete your sentence. Then the teacher shows the better way. Confidence first.
  • Bengali allowed for explaining, not for answeringIf you do not understand a word, ask in Bengali. Reply in English, however broken.
  • Recorded on your phone once a monthYou hear yourself in month one and month three. The difference is the report card.

Common questions

Before you message us.

I cannot speak a single sentence. Can I still join?

Yes. About a third of every beginner batch starts exactly there. The first two weeks are only listening and repeating. Nobody is asked to make a speech on day one.

I am 45 and a housewife. Will I be in a class with college students?

No. The 11 am batch is women only and most are between 30 and 55. If that time does not suit you, tell us and we will find a mixed batch where you will be comfortable.

How does the free demo class work?

Message or call with your name and the time you prefer. Come and sit through one full class of the level we think fits you. At the end you decide. There is no form to fill and nobody follows up more than once.

What if I miss classes?

Each lesson runs in two or three batches a week, so you can attend the same lesson in another batch that week. If you travel for a month, we pause your course and you join the next batch at the same point.

Do you give a certificate?

Yes, a centre certificate with the level and attendance after the course. We say plainly that it is our own certificate, not a university one. Employers care about how you speak in the interview, and that is what we work on.

Is there a batch for IELTS or for studying abroad?

Not at present. We do spoken English and interview preparation and we do them properly. If you need IELTS coaching we will tell you honestly and suggest you look elsewhere.

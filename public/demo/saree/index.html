Handloom

We buy from the loom, not the wholesaler.

Twice a month someone from the shop takes the train to Phulia and Shantipur. We sit with the weaver families we have known since the eighties, see what is on the loom and pay on the spot. It means fewer middlemen, a fairer price for the weaver and a saree you can trace back to a house and a name.

Everything marked handloom in the shop carries a tag with the weaver’s village. Ask us and we will tell you more.

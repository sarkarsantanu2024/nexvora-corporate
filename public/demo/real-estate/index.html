Brokerage and charges

What we charge, said plainly.

Pradip Bhattacharya started this office in 2011 after ten years with a builder in Salt Lake. The rule from day one: tell the customer the fee before the first visit, and never add anything to it later.

  • Renting a flat: one month’s rent

    Paid once, after you sign the agreement and get the keys. If the rent is ₹20,000, our fee is ₹20,000. The owner pays us the same from their side. Nothing is due before you move in.

  • Buying a flat: 1% of the sale price

    Paid at registration, not before. On a ₹60 lakh flat that is ₹60,000. It covers every visit, the price talks with the owner, and sitting with you through the paperwork.

  • What is not ours

    Stamp duty, registration fee, society transfer charge, lawyer’s fee and the police verification fee are paid directly by you to those offices. We tell you the amounts in writing before you decide.

  • No site visit charge, no listing charge

    Seeing flats costs nothing. If you do not take a flat through us, you owe us nothing. We make our fee only when a deal closes.

How a site visit works

From first message to keys, five steps.

  1. Step 1

    Send us what you need

    Area, budget, BHK, and when you want to move. One WhatsApp message is enough. We reply within working hours, usually within the hour.

  2. Step 2

    We send a shortlist

    Three to six flats with photos, exact rent or price, floor, and the maintenance amount. No flat we have not visited ourselves.

  3. Step 3

    Visit, on a day that suits you

    We fix a time with the owner and come with you. Weekends and evenings are fine. Two or three flats in one trip, so you can compare them.

  4. Step 4

    We talk price with the owner

    You tell us what you can pay; we take it to the owner. Most rentals settle in a day. Sales take a week or two.

  5. Step 5

    Paperwork and keys

    Rent agreement on stamp paper, or sale deed with the lawyer. We sit with you at the registry office. Our fee is paid only at this step.

Documents checklist

Keep these ready and the deal closes faster.

Photocopies are fine for the first visit. Originals are needed only on the day of signing.

If you are buying

For the sale deed and, if you need one, the home loan.

  • PAN card and AadhaarOf every person whose name goes on the deed.
  • Passport-size photosFour each. The registry office keeps two.
  • Last 6 months’ bank statementNeeded by the bank for a loan, and by the lawyer to show the source of funds.
  • Salary slips or ITR for 2 yearsOnly if you are taking a loan.
  • From the seller, we collectOriginal deed, mutation certificate, tax receipts, society NOC and the completion certificate. We check all of it before you pay a token.

If you are renting

For the agreement and the police verification the owner must file.

  • Aadhaar and one more IDVoter card, passport or driving licence.
  • Two passport-size photosOf every adult who will live in the flat.
  • Office ID or offer letterOwners in New Town and Salt Lake almost always ask for this.
  • Security depositUsually two months’ rent, sometimes three in Salt Lake. Returned when you leave, minus any damage.
  • Stamp paper for the agreementWe arrange it. 11-month agreement, about ₹1,200 including notary, split between you and the owner.

How a matter proceeds

From the first meeting to the court date.

  1. Day 1

    You tell us the problem

    Thirty minutes at the chamber. We read your papers, ask questions, and tell you plainly whether a case is worth filing, what the usual steps are, and roughly how long it takes.

  2. Within 3 days

    Fee quoted in writing

    You get a WhatsApp message with the fee for the whole matter, what it covers, and what is paid at each stage. Court fees and stamp duty are shown separately. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. Week 1 to 2

    Drafting and your signature

    The notice, plaint, petition or deed is drafted and read out to you in Bengali or English, whichever you prefer. You sign only after you understand every line.

  4. Filing

    Filed, and you get the case number

    We file at the right court and send you the case number and the first date. From then on you receive a message after every hearing with what happened and what comes next.

  5. Each hearing

    Updates after every date

    You do not have to come to court for most dates. When your presence is needed, we tell you a week before, and what to carry.

Common questions

Things people ask before they come.

Do I need an appointment?

It helps. Send a WhatsApp message with your name and the kind of matter, and we give you a time slot so you are not waiting. Walk-ins between 5 pm and 9 pm are seen if the advocate is free.

Can you tell me on the phone whether I have a case?

Not properly. Most matters depend on the papers. Bring them to the chamber and you will get a straight answer in thirty minutes. If there is no case worth filing, we say so and you pay only the consultation fee.

How long does a mutual consent divorce take?

Usually six to eight months from the first motion to the decree, with the six-month waiting period in between. The court can shorten the wait in some situations. We explain this at the first meeting.

Will I have to come to court on every date?

No. For most dates the advocate appears alone. You need to be present for evidence, for certain statements, and on the day of a final order. We tell you a week in advance.

Do you take cases outside Kolkata?

Matters at Alipore, Sealdah, Barasat and Howrah courts, yes. Other districts only when there is a strong reason, because travel adds to your cost.

Can I pay in parts?

Yes. Court matters are usually paid in two or three parts: at filing, at evidence, and at argument. The split is written into the fee quote. UPI, bank transfer and cash are all accepted, with a receipt.

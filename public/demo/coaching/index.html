Subjects

Three subjects, taught by subject teachers

No one here teaches everything. Each subject has a teacher whose degree is in that subject.

Mathematics

Class 5 to 12

Arithmetic to calculus. Every chapter ends with a 20-sum sheet, and the child does not move on until the sheet is clean. Board students get a formula booklet we print ourselves.

Science

Physical Science, Life Science, PCM

Diagrams drawn on the board and in the copy, not photocopied. Numericals practised till they are routine. For Class 11-12, Physics and Chemistry run as separate batches.

English

Class 5 to 12

Grammar, unseen passages, letter and report writing, and the textbook. Bengali-medium students get extra speaking and writing practice because that is where marks are lost.

Our method

How results actually happen

There is no secret. Results come from a child being seen every week, not once before the exam.

  1. 01

    Fifteen students to a batch

    Small enough that the teacher knows who is stuck by the second day, not the second month.

  2. 02

    Test every Saturday

    Thirty minutes on the week's chapters. Copies returned Monday with corrections, not just a number.

  3. 03

    Doubt hour, every day

    From 8 to 9 pm a teacher sits in Room 2. Any student from any batch can walk in with a copy.

  4. 04

    Monthly report to parents

    Attendance, test marks and two lines from the teacher, sent on WhatsApp on the 1st. Parents are asked to come in twice a year.

  5. 05

    Board-pattern test series

    From December, Class 10 and 12 students sit six full-length papers under exam time, in the exam hall seating.

Saturday, 5 pm

The test is the lesson

Every Saturday the week's chapters go on a thirty-minute paper. The copies come back on Monday with the working corrected in red, not just a mark at the top. A child who sees the same mistake twice stops making it.

Parents ask

Before you call

My child is in a Bengali-medium school. Is that a problem?

No. Around half our students are from Bengali-medium schools. Foundation and Class 9-10 batches are split by medium so the teacher can explain in Bengali where needed. Notes are given in both.

Can we join only for one subject?

Yes, from Class 9 onward. Single-subject fees are in the table above. Class 5 to 8 is taught as one combined batch because the three subjects share the timing.

What happens if my child misses a Saturday test?

The same paper is given on Monday in the doubt hour. Marks still go into the monthly report, so parents see the full picture.

Do you teach the school's own textbook or your own notes?

The board textbook first, always. Our printed notes are a summary and extra sums, not a replacement. For ICSE and CBSE we follow the prescribed books of those boards.

Is there a batch for students who want JEE or WBJEE?

There is an optional problem-solving class on Sunday afternoons for Class 11-12 at ₹600 a month. It is a supplement, not a full entrance course. We say that plainly so nobody is misled.

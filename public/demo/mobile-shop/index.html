EMI and exchange

Pay monthly, or trade in the phone you have.

1

Credit card EMI

3, 6, 9 or 12 months on most bank cards. No-cost EMI on phones above ₹10,000 when the brand runs an offer, which is most months. Takes about five minutes at the counter.

  • We swipe the card once; the bank splits it into EMIs within a week.
  • Processing fee, if any, is shown to you before you sign.
2

Finance without a credit card

Bajaj, HDB and others. Approval on the spot in most cases, and you walk out with the phone the same day.

  • Bring: Aadhaar, PAN, a cancelled cheque or bank passbook, and the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar for the OTP.
  • Down payment: usually 10 to 20 percent of the price. The rest in 6 to 12 EMIs.
  • Salaried or self-employed both work. No hidden charges; the total you will pay is shown before you sign.
3

Old phone exchange

Bring your old phone. We check it in ten minutes and give you a fixed price off the new one.

  • What we check: screen (cracks, dead spots), battery health, camera, charging, speaker, and whether it switches on and opens with your pattern.
  • What raises the value: original box and charger, no scratches on the screen, battery above 80 percent, a phone less than three years old.
  • What lowers it: cracked screen, swollen battery, water damage, a locked account that is not signed out.
  • Your data: we help you back up and sign out, then wipe the phone in front of you.
Example: a two-year-old ₹15,000 phone in good condition usually fetches ₹3,500 to ₹6,000 off the new phone. Flagships fetch more.

Bill and warranty

What you get with every sale.

  • GST bill in your name

    Printed and sent on WhatsApp. You need it for the brand warranty and for insurance claims.

  • Brand warranty, one year

    Sealed box, IMEI matched to the bill. For a claim we send the phone to the brand service centre for you.

  • Repair warranty, 3 months

    On the part and the work. Same fault again, we fix it free. Physical damage and water are not covered.

  • 7-day bring-back

    Dead on arrival, wrong colour, or a fault in the first week: come back with the box and we sort it out with the brand.

We sell what we can stand behind. Every phone comes with a GST bill in your name, the sealed box, and the brand warranty. If something is wrong in the first seven days, bring it back and we deal with the brand for you. On any day there are forty or more phones in stock to pick up and try.

Common questions

Things people ask at the counter.

Is the price the same as online?

Usually within a few hundred rupees, and often the same during bank offers. You get the phone today, a bill in your name, and a shop to walk into if something goes wrong.

Do you use original parts for repair?

We offer both: original parts where the brand supplies them, and good compatible parts that cost less. We tell you which is which and the price of each before we start.

Can I wait while the phone is repaired?

Yes. Screen and battery take one to two hours. Sit downstairs, or leave it and we message you when it is ready.

What do I bring for finance?

Aadhaar, PAN, a bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and the mobile number linked to Aadhaar for the OTP. Salaried or self-employed both work.

Do you buy old phones without an exchange?

Yes, for cash, but the price is lower than exchange value. Bring the box and bill if you have them.

Do you do recharges and SIM cards?

Yes. All networks, prepaid and postpaid, and new SIMs with Aadhaar verification at the counter.

Before the test

Fasting rules and other things worth knowing.

Half the repeat visits we see are because of a cup of tea. Read this the night before.

Fasting means 10 to 12 hours

Finish dinner by 9 pm and come between 6:30 and 9 am. Plain water is fine, as much as you like. No tea, coffee, biscuits, chewing gum or paan. Fasting is needed for fasting sugar, lipid profile and some packages, not for CBC, thyroid or vitamins.

Medicines

Take your pressure and thyroid tablets as usual with water. For sugar tablets or insulin, ask your doctor; most say take them after the fasting sample. Carry the medicine strip so we can note the name.

PP (post-meal) sugar

Eat your normal breakfast right after the fasting sample, note the time, and come back exactly two hours later. Do not skip or shrink the meal; the test is meant to see a normal day.

Urine and stool samples

Collect the first urine of the morning in the sterile cup we give you (or buy one from any chemist for ₹10). Mid-stream, not the first few drops. Bring it within two hours. Women should avoid the test during periods unless the doctor says otherwise.

Ultrasound of the abdomen

Six hours empty stomach, and drink four to five glasses of water one hour before so the bladder is full. Do not pass urine until the scan is done. For a pelvic scan the full bladder matters most.

ECG

Nothing special. Avoid heavy exercise, tea and smoking for one hour before. Wear a shirt or kurta that opens at the front. The test takes five minutes and the report is ready in fifteen.

Who checks your report

A pathologist reads every report before it leaves the lab.

Dr. Sumana Ghosh, MD (Pathology), has run the lab since it opened in 2014. She checks every report personally and signs it. Abnormal smears are looked at under the microscope, not just by the machine.

The lab runs a fully automated haematology and biochemistry analyser, calibrated every morning before the first sample. Controls are run twice a day and the records are kept in a register you can ask to see. Samples go into the machine within two hours of collection.

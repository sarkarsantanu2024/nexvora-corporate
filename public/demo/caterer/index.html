How booking works

From the first message to the last plate.

  1. 1Day 1

    Send us the date, the venue and the guest count

    On WhatsApp or by phone. We check the date is free and send a written quote the same evening, package by package.

  2. 2Within a week

    Come for a tasting

    Pick a Saturday afternoon. You taste 8 to 10 dishes from your package at our Behala kitchen and change what you like. The tasting is free once you have booked, ₹1,500 before.

  3. 3To confirm

    Pay 50% advance

    Half the estimated bill, by UPI or bank transfer. You get a signed menu, the plate count, the counter list and the final per-plate price on one page.

  4. 47 days before

    Confirm the final guest count

    You can move the count up or down by 10% till a week before. We cook for 5% more than the number you give, at no charge.

  5. 5The day

    We arrive four hours before the first guest

    Set up, cook, serve, clear and wash. The balance is paid after the last guest has eaten, in cash or UPI, against the GST bill.

What is included

Everything on this list comes with the per-plate price.

  • Serving staff

    One server for every 25 guests, in uniform. A captain who stays with your family for the evening.

  • Crockery and cutlery

    Ceramic plates, steel cutlery, glass tumblers. Leaf plates and clay bhaar if you prefer the old way.

  • Buffet counters and warmers

    Skirted tables, chafing dishes with fuel, and a decorated buffet line in your colour.

  • Dining tables and chairs

    For 50% of the guests at one time, with covers. More on request at ₹40 a chair.

  • Cooking on site

    Our gas, our cooks, our utensils. We can cook in a courtyard, a garage or the venue kitchen.

  • Clearing and washing

    Plates cleared from the table, the kitchen area washed down, and rubbish taken away in our van.

  • Drinking water and ice

    Packaged water for every guest, ice boxes for the drinks you bring, and glasses washed and refilled.

  • Written bill

    A GST invoice with every item listed. Nothing added on the day that was not agreed before.

Not included, so you know

We would rather say it here than surprise you later.

  • Hall or venue rent
  • Decoration beyond the buffet line
  • Soft drinks and bottled juices (we serve what you buy)
  • Transport outside Kolkata: ₹18 per km each way
  • Guests above the confirmed count, billed per plate

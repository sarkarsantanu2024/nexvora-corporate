The old Mantra Kidz site was template text, so almost all the copy on the new
site was written by us. Nothing below is a known fact. Each line is something
the school must either confirm or change before launch. Everything is editable
in the dashboard under **Website** (the section name is given in brackets), so
no code change is needed.

What we do know and did not need to ask: the name and the two lines under it,
the four classes and their ages, 12 seats per class, the address, the phone
number, and the two testimonials (Pritam Das, Biswajit Saha), used word for word.

## 6. Bengali and Hindi

The whole site is also in Bengali (`/bn/...`) and Hindi (`/hi/...`). Both were
written by us from the English, in everyday words, and **neither has been
read by the school**. Everything in section 2 and 3 above applies to the
translations too: a statement to be confirmed in English is the same
statement in the other two languages. Each language is edited separately in
the panel.

Please have a Bengali and a Hindi reader at the school go through the site
once. Points we are least sure of:

- **The address in Bengali and Hindi script.** We wrote "দক্ষিণদাঁড়ি রোড" and "दक्षिणदारी रोड" for Dakshindari Road. Use the spelling on the school's own signboard. [Whole site, Contact details]
- **The school's name is left in English letters** ("Mantra Kidz", "Mind Mantra Abacus") in both languages, to match the logo. If the school writes its name in Bengali or Hindi script anywhere, tell us the spelling.
- **Class names** are written as they are spoken: প্লে গ্রুপ, নার্সারি, এলকেজি, ইউকেজি and प्ले ग्रुप, नर्सरी, एलकेजी, यूकेजी.
- **The two testimonials are left in English**, exactly as given. We did not translate someone else's words.
- **Teachers are called "শিক্ষিকা" / "शिक्षिकाएँ"** (women teachers) in most places, as is usual for a play school. If the school has men teaching too, say so and we will change it to the neutral word.
- **"Franchise" words:** লগ্নি (investment), চুক্তি (agreement), হেড অফিস; निवेश, समझौता, मुख्य कार्यालय. The school may prefer the English words it uses with enquirers.
- **Menu labels are shortened** so the menu fits: "ক্লাস" / "कक्षाएँ" for Programmes, "ছবি" / "तस्वीरें" for Gallery, "আমাদের কথা" / "हमारे बारे में" for About.
- The wording of the four classes in Bengali and Hindi (descriptions and activity points) comes from the programmes data, not from us; check it in Programmes in the dashboard.
- Ages, phone numbers and counts use the digits 0-9 in every language, on purpose.
- Notices and gallery captions are typed by the school and appear as typed, in whatever language that is.
